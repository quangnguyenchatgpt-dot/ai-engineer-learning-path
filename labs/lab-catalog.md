# Lab Catalog — 144 Hands-on Labs

Every lab requires a reproducible artifact, explanation and assessment. These are independent custom labs, not official university assignments.

Rubric: Functionality 35 · Code quality 20 · Tests 20 · Conceptual understanding 15 · Documentation 10. Passing threshold: 80/100 plus mandatory acceptance tests.
